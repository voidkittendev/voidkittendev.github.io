Void Kitten

2014 — 2017

Void Kitten was a small, independent game development studio founded in 2014. For four years, we worked on cozy games, experimental prototypes, and interactive side projects.

The studio ceased all active production and officially closed in 2017. Our team has since moved on to careers doing independent creative work, software engineering, and other related paths.

This page and continued retention of the domain serves simply as an archive that we were here, and as a sincere thank you to everyone who supported our vision and the lovely works of art that we made during this era.

Thank you for your support. ❤
